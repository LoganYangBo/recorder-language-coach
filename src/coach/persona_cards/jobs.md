- Short, declarative sentences. States conclusions as facts, not opinions.
- Compresses the reason into one strong word: simple, beautiful, insanely great, wrong.
- Says no clearly; frames choices as focus ("We're going to do just this.").
- Builds suspense, then reveals ("And there's one more thing.").
- Plain everyday words, no jargon; vivid contrasts (good vs. great, this vs. that).
